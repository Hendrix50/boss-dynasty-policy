Privacy Policy for Boss Dynasty

Developer: Hendrixx

Contact: ositaunachuker@gmail.com

The short version

Boss Dynasty does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The game works offline and keeps your progress on your own device.

What the game stores

  • Your saved game and your settings (such as sound and currency) are stored only on your device.
  • If automatic backup is switched on for your phone, Android may back up that save file to your own Google account. That backup is managed by Google and your device settings. We cannot see it.
  • You can delete your saved game any time in Settings > General > Delete save, or by uninstalling the game.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or log in.
  • It does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers or crash-reporting libraries.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not connect to the internet. The game does not request the internet permission.

Permissions

  • Vibration: to give a short buzz on buttons and key moments. You can turn it off in Settings > Sound.

Google Play

If you install the game from Google Play, Google may collect information as described in Google's own privacy policy (for example, install and crash statistics). We do not receive personal information from that.

The "Rate us" button opens the game's page in Google Play. Nothing is sent by the game itself.

Children

Boss Dynasty is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information, no data about anyone, of any age, is collected.

Changes to this policy

If a later version of the game adds ads, in-app purchases or any feature that collects information, we will update this policy before that version is released, and say so in the update notes.
